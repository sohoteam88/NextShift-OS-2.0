# Content Engine Examples

## Example 1: 90-Day Plan for Health Coach

**Input:** "Create a 90-day content plan for my weight management coaching brand. Target: 30-45 year old working moms in Malaysia. Posting 1x/day."

**Expected output:**

Content pillars: 1) 营养教育, 2) 真实故事, 3) 专业权威, 4) 产品/服务, 5) 社群互动

90 posts allocated by ratio:
- 36 Education (40%): meal prep tips, myth busting, nutrient basics, habit science
- 18 Story (20%): my transformation, client journeys, daily life, struggles + wins
- 18 Authority (20%): credentials, results data, expert interviews, case studies
- 9 Offer (10%): consultation invite, program details, limited spots, testimonial + CTA
- 9 Community (10%): Q&A, polls, challenges, member shoutouts, live sessions

Week 1 detailed plan:
| Day | Type | Core Idea | Hook | FB | IG | TikTok | 小红书 | CTA |
|-----|------|-----------|------|----|----|--------|--------|-----|
| Mon | Education | 3 breakfast mistakes | "你的早餐可能在让你变胖" | Long post | Carousel 5 slides | 30s talking head | 笔记 + 关键词 | Save + comment |
| Tue | Story | My 15kg journey | "2年前的我不敢照镜子" | Personal post | Reel before/after | 45s storytelling | 图文故事 | DM "想知道" |
...

Repurposing workflow: 1 long-form idea → FB post → IG carousel → TikTok script → 小红书 note.

## Example 2: Content Pillars Only

**Input:** "I'm a fitness coach but don't know what to post about. Help me find my content pillars."

**Expected output:** Based on positioning: 1) 居家运动 (home workouts), 2) 营养搭配 (nutrition pairing), 3) 习惯养成 (habit building), 4) 学员变化 (client transformations), 5) 常见误区 (myth busting). Each pillar with 5 topic ideas and 3 hook templates.

## When NOT to Use This Skill

- User needs **AI to write the actual post** → use `ai/ai-content-generator`
- User needs **personal brand positioning first** → use `growth/personal-brand`
- User needs **video scripts** → use `verticals/ai-video-engine`
