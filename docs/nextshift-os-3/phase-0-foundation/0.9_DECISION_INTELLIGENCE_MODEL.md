# Document 0.9 - Decision Intelligence Model

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

## Purpose

This document defines how NextShift transforms business understanding into intelligent business decisions.

Decision Intelligence is the cognitive layer between Business Intelligence and Execution.

It determines what should happen next.

This model is shared by every AI Agent, Recommendation Engine, Strategy Engine, Opportunity Engine, and AI Coach.

## Definition

Decision Intelligence is the systematic process of evaluating business opportunities, risks, priorities, and available resources to determine the most valuable next action.

Business Intelligence explains the business.

Decision Intelligence decides what to do about it.

## Core Principle

Understanding creates awareness.

Decision Intelligence creates action.

AI should never execute because something happened.

AI should execute because it understands what matters most.

## Decision Pipeline

```text
Business Understanding
  -> Insights
  -> Opportunities
  -> Risks
  -> Priorities
  -> Recommendations
  -> Discussion
  -> Decision
  -> Execution
```

## Stage 1 - Business Understanding

Decision making begins only after sufficient business understanding exists.

Inputs include:

- Business Twin
- Business Intelligence
- Current Signals
- Historical Performance
- Strategic Objectives

Without understanding, no recommendation should be generated.

## Stage 2 - Insight Evaluation

AI evaluates:

- What changed?
- Why did it change?
- What does it mean?
- What is the business impact?

Only meaningful insights move forward.

## Stage 3 - Opportunity Discovery

Every insight may reveal one or more opportunities.

Each opportunity should estimate:

- Business value
- Strategic importance
- Time sensitivity
- Resource requirements

Opportunities represent possible improvements.

They are not yet recommendations.

## Stage 4 - Risk Assessment

AI evaluates potential risks.

Examples include:

- Revenue loss
- Customer dissatisfaction
- Operational disruption
- Brand damage
- Resource constraints
- Competitive threats

Every recommendation should include associated risks.

## Stage 5 - Priority Evaluation

AI prioritizes opportunities using multiple dimensions.

Evaluation factors include:

- Strategic Alignment
- Expected Business Impact
- Urgency
- Confidence
- Resource Availability
- Risk Level
- Learning Potential

Priority is dynamic.

It changes as the Business Twin evolves.

## Stage 6 - Recommendation Generation

Recommendations should contain:

- Objective
- Expected Outcome
- Supporting Evidence
- Risks
- Alternatives
- Estimated Effort
- Confidence
- Recommended Next Step

Recommendations should always be ranked.

## Stage 7 - Discussion

The entrepreneur collaborates with AI.

Discussion may:

- Refine priorities
- Add context
- Reject assumptions
- Modify recommendations
- Introduce constraints

Discussion improves decision quality.

## Stage 8 - Decision

A decision records:

- Selected option
- Decision rationale
- Expected outcome
- Decision owner
- Timestamp

Decision Records become part of the Business Twin.

## Stage 9 - Execution

Approved decisions become executable work.

Execution should remain aligned with the original objective.

Execution Capabilities never redefine strategic intent.

## Stage 10 - Reflection

AI evaluates:

- Did the recommendation work?
- Why?
- What surprised us?
- Which assumptions were incorrect?
- What should change next time?

Reflection closes the decision loop.

## Decision Principles

Every significant decision should be:

- Context-aware
- Explainable
- Evidence-based
- Goal-oriented
- Risk-aware
- Adaptive
- Learning-driven

## Decision Hierarchy

AI evaluates priorities in the following order:

1. Business Survival
2. Customer Value
3. Strategic Goals
4. Revenue Growth
5. Operational Efficiency
6. Long-Term Learning

Lower priorities should never compromise higher priorities without explicit approval.

## Decision Quality

A high-quality decision is one that:

- Aligns with business goals.
- Uses available evidence.
- Considers uncertainty.
- Explains trade-offs.
- Produces measurable outcomes.
- Improves future learning.

Decision quality is more important than execution speed.

## Relationship to the Business Twin

The Business Twin provides business understanding.

Decision Intelligence provides business judgment.

Execution Capabilities provide business action.

Learning improves all three.

## Success Criteria

Decision Intelligence succeeds when:

- Entrepreneurs know what matters most.
- AI recommends the highest-value actions.
- Decisions become more consistent.
- Outcomes improve over time.
- Business knowledge compounds.

## Guiding Principle

The purpose of AI is not to make more decisions.

The purpose of AI is to help entrepreneurs make better decisions.
