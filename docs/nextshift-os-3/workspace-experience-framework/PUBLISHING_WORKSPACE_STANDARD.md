# Publishing Workspace Standard

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

Last Updated: 2026-07-02

---

## Purpose

Define the architecture, workflow, and governance for the Publishing Workspace in NextShift OS.

---

## Mission

The Publishing Workspace is the final execution layer of the AI Business Workspace.

It transforms approved content into measurable business outcomes through scheduling, publishing, monitoring, and continuous learning.

---

## End-To-End Workflow

```text
Business Brain
  -> Content Intelligence
  -> Content / Visual / Video Workspace
  -> Publishing Workspace
  -> Platform Delivery
  -> Analytics
  -> Learning System
```

## AI Responsibilities

AI should:

- Recommend publish timing
- Detect publishing conflicts
- Recommend platform mix
- Predict expected performance
- Detect underperforming posts
- Recommend optimization actions

---

## Governance Rules

Publishing Workspace must:

- Consume approved assets only.
- Preserve audit history.
- Record all publishing events.
- Feed Analytics and Learning System automatically.
- Never bypass approval workflows.

---

## Success Criteria

Users can schedule, approve, publish, monitor, and optimize multi-platform campaigns from one workspace while every publishing event contributes to the Business Learning Loop.
