# @nextshift/business-brain

Business understanding package for NextShift OS.

## Responsibilities

- Provide unified business context.
- Serve Business Twin context to intelligent components.
- Define ports for Business Memory, Story Vault, and Knowledge Graph.

## Non-responsibilities

- Does not make recommendations.
- Does not execute workflows.
- Does not own infrastructure.
- Does not access database directly in this bootstrap.
- Does not implement full Business Twin logic yet.

## Architecture Rule

Business Brain understands the business.

Decision Brain decides what should happen next.
