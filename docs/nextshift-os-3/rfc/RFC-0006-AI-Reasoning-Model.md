# RFC-0006 - AI Reasoning Model

Status: Approved

Current document: [0.6 AI Reasoning Model](../phase-0-foundation/0.6_AI_REASONING_MODEL.md)

## Summary

Define how AI transforms business information into business intelligence.

## Decision

NextShift AI reasons through Business Signals, Business Context, Business Understanding, Insights, Opportunities, Recommendations, Discussion, Decision, Execution, Reflection, and Learning.

## Constitution Impact

This RFC standardizes how all AI Agents observe, understand, recommend, and learn.

## Approval Criteria

- Business understanding precedes business action.
- Recommendations include confidence.
- Low-confidence states trigger clarifying questions.
- Reflection updates Business Twin, Business Memory, Knowledge Graph, and Recommendation Models.
