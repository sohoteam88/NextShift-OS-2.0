## Notes

- “Opportunity proxy” is used where current runtime computes next-best-action, mission, or recommended work instead of a first-class `opportunities[]` list.
- “Readiness proxy” is used where progress percent, activation level, or mission state is acting like readiness, even if the source does not explicitly use that term.
