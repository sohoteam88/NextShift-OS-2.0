## Revision 01

Remove Confidence Score

### Previous

Current Stage

Current Bottleneck

Today's Mission

Expected Outcome

Confidence: 92%

### Problem

Users do not understand:

92% confidence in what?

The score creates artificial precision.

Trust decreases.

### Decision

Remove confidence percentage entirely.

Replace with:

Priority Level

Values:

- Critical
- High
- Normal

### Example

Priority

High

Recommended Next Action

Create Your First Lead Capture System

- Content System
- Funnel Setup

Current Gap:

- Why now?
- Why not something else?
