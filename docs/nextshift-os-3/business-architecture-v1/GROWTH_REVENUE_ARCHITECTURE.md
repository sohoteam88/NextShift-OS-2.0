# Growth & Revenue Architecture

Version: 1.0

Status: Implemented

Last Updated: 2026-07-08

---

## Purpose

Define how NextShift supports measurable business growth and revenue workflows.

Growth & Revenue converts approved business and creative direction into funnels, traffic, CRM activity, WhatsApp revenue activity, follow-up, conversion, and measurement.

---

## Responsibilities

| Area | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Funnels | Define offer paths, landing flow, conversion points, and follow-up steps |
| Traffic | Plan channels, audience, content distribution, and campaign activity |
| CRM | Manage leads, qualification, opportunities, and customer state |
| WhatsApp Revenue | Support conversational follow-up, sales messaging, and customer engagement |
| Follow-Up | Track next actions, reminders, and lead progression |
| Conversion | Measure opportunity movement and revenue outcomes |
| Forecasts | Review expected revenue, pipeline health, and risk |
| Analytics | Convert results into insights and learning signals |

---

## Inputs

Growth & Revenue reads:

- Business Foundation identity and goals
- Business Brain customer and offer understanding
- Decision Engine priorities
- Creative Studio publishing packages
- workflow outputs
- customer and revenue signals

---

## Outputs

Growth & Revenue produces:

- funnel plans
- traffic plans
- qualified lead state
- opportunity assessments
- campaign execution records
- follow-up tasks
- revenue forecast reviews
- analytics insight reviews
- learning feedback

---

## Boundary

Growth & Revenue executes measurable business activity.

It does not own core business identity, final recommendation logic, creative asset generation, or platform runtime behavior.
