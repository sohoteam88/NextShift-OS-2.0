# RFC-0002 - Business Ontology

Status: Approved

Current document: [0.2 Business Ontology](../phase-0-foundation/0.2_BUSINESS_ONTOLOGY.md)

## Summary

Define the official language of NextShift OS 3.0.

## Decision

NextShift uses a shared ontology for Business, Business Twin, Story, Memory, Knowledge, Recommendation, Decision, Capability, Agent, Insight, Opportunity, Reflection, Learning, Execution, Signal, and Goal.

## Constitution Impact

This RFC establishes the shared vocabulary for future architecture, product, AI, and implementation documents.

## Approval Criteria

- Prevents conflicting terminology.
- Keeps business truth centered on the Business Twin.
- Gives every future capability a shared conceptual map.
