# NextShift Implementation Master Roadmap

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

Last Updated: 2026-07-06

---

## Purpose

Translate the approved MVP 1.0 standards into a practical implementation sequence with measurable milestones.

---

## Delivery Principle

Implement from the inside out:

1. Business Intelligence
2. Workspaces
3. Publishing
4. Learning
5. Optimization

No phase begins until the previous phase has passed governance and release gates.

---

## Phase 1 - Business OS Foundation

## Milestone 1

- Business Profile
- Business Twin
- CRM
- Campaign
- Revenue
- Analytics

## Milestone 2

- Decision Brain
- Business Brain
- AI Workflow
- Knowledge Graph
- Story Vault

Exit Criteria:

- Business understanding complete.
- Explainable recommendations available.
