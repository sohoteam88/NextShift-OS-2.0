# Document 0.7 - Business Intelligence Model

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

## Purpose

This document defines how NextShift transforms raw business information into business intelligence.

The Business Intelligence Model serves as the cognitive framework that guides every AI recommendation, strategic analysis, and business decision.

It provides the common reasoning model shared across the entire platform.

## Core Principle

Data alone does not create intelligence.

Intelligence emerges when data is interpreted within the context of the Business Twin.

Every AI capability should contribute to improving business intelligence.

## Intelligence Pipeline

```text
Business Signals
  -> Business Context
  -> Business Understanding
  -> Business Insights
  -> Business Opportunities
  -> Recommendations
  -> Business Decisions
  -> Execution
  -> Reflection
  -> Learning
  -> Business Twin Evolution
```

## Stage 1 - Business Signals

Signals are raw observations collected from the business.

Examples include:

- Sales transactions
- Customer conversations
- Marketing metrics
- Financial updates
- Operational events

Signals have no meaning until interpreted.

## Stage 2 - Business Context

Signals are combined with historical knowledge stored in the Business Twin.

Context includes:

- Business goals
- Strategy
- Customer segments
- Historical performance
- Previous decisions
- Constraints

Context transforms isolated events into meaningful situations.

## Stage 3 - Business Understanding

AI develops an explanation of the current business state.

Understanding answers:

- What happened?
- Why did it happen?
- What changed?
- What is affected?

Understanding is the foundation of all future reasoning.

## Stage 4 - Business Insights

Insights identify meaningful patterns.

An insight should explain something previously unknown or not immediately obvious.

Insights should be evidence-based.

## Stage 5 - Business Opportunities

AI identifies possible improvements.

Each opportunity should include:

- Expected business value
- Strategic alignment
- Estimated effort
- Associated risks

Not every opportunity becomes a recommendation.

## Stage 6 - Recommendations

Recommendations prioritize opportunities.

Every recommendation should include:

- Reasoning
- Supporting evidence
- Expected impact
- Confidence level
- Risks
- Alternative options

Recommendations should always be explainable.

## Stage 7 - Business Decisions

The entrepreneur evaluates recommendations.

Decisions become part of the Business Twin and influence future reasoning.

## Stage 8 - Execution

Approved decisions are executed through platform capabilities.

Execution should always remain aligned with the original business objective.

## Stage 9 - Reflection

AI compares expected outcomes with actual results.

Reflection identifies:

- Success factors
- Failure factors
- Unexpected outcomes
- Lessons learned

## Stage 10 - Learning

Learning permanently improves:

- Business Twin
- Business Memory
- Knowledge Graph
- Recommendation quality
- Strategic understanding

Every completed cycle should strengthen future decision-making.

## Design Principles

Business intelligence should be:

- Context-aware
- Explainable
- Evidence-based
- Continuously evolving
- Business outcome focused
- Shared across all AI Agents

## Success Criteria

The Business Intelligence Model succeeds when:

- AI understands the business more accurately.
- Recommendations become more relevant.
- Decisions become more effective.
- Entrepreneurs achieve better business outcomes over time.

## Guiding Principle

NextShift does not optimize for generating more content.

NextShift optimizes for generating better business intelligence.
