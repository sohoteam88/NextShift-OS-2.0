# Business Twin Contract

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

## Purpose

This document defines the contractual responsibilities of the Business Twin.

The Business Twin Contract specifies the capabilities, responsibilities, constraints, and guarantees that every implementation of the Business Twin must satisfy.

This contract is implementation-independent.

It applies regardless of programming language, database technology, storage engine, or AI provider.

## Mission

The Business Twin is the authoritative cognitive representation of a business.

Its purpose is to provide a continuously evolving understanding that supports reasoning, decision-making, execution, and learning.

## Contract Principles

The Business Twin must be:

- Persistent
- Explainable
- Context-aware
- Continuously evolving
- Shared across the platform
- Independent of implementation technology

## Responsibilities

The Business Twin shall:

- Maintain current business understanding.
- Maintain long-term business context.
- Integrate business knowledge from multiple domains.
- Support AI reasoning.
- Support business decision-making.
- Preserve historical continuity.
- Improve through learning.

## Required Capabilities

The Business Twin must provide the ability to maintain or access the following areas of business understanding.

### Identity

Maintain:

- Vision
- Mission
- Values
- Brand DNA
- Positioning

### Strategy

Maintain:

- Goals
- Objectives
- Priorities
- Constraints
- Strategic initiatives

### Business Context

Maintain:

- Products
- Services
- Customers
- Channels
- Team
- Operations

### Knowledge

Provide access to:

- Knowledge Graph
- Business Rules
- SOPs
- Playbooks
- Relationships

### Memory

Provide access to:

- Stories
- Decisions
- Preferences
- Historical context
- Lessons learned

### Learning

Accept updates from:

- Learning System
- Reflection Engine
- User corrections
- Approved business events

## Guarantees

The Business Twin guarantees:

- A single authoritative business understanding.
- Consistent business context across all AI Agents.
- Traceable business reasoning.
- Preservation of business history.
- Continuous improvement through learning.

## Prohibited Responsibilities

The Business Twin must not:

- Execute workflows.
- Generate UI.
- Replace the Decision Brain.
- Own execution capabilities.
- Store presentation logic.
- Become a reporting dashboard.

## Integration Contract

The Business Twin receives information from:

- Business Brain
- Story Vault
- Business Memory
- Knowledge Graph
- Learning System
- Business Events

The Business Twin provides information to:

- Decision Brain
- AI Agents
- Recommendation Engine
- AI Coach
- Analytics
- Execution Layer

## Update Rules

The Business Twin may be updated only through approved business events, validated learning, user-confirmed corrections, or authorized system processes.

All updates should preserve historical traceability.

## Success Criteria

The Business Twin Contract is satisfied when:

- Every AI component shares the same business understanding.
- Recommendations remain context-aware.
- Business knowledge compounds over time.
- New capabilities integrate without duplicating business truth.
- Implementation details can evolve without changing the Business Twin interface.

## Guiding Principle

The Business Twin is not a database model.

It is the platform's cognitive contract with every intelligent component.
