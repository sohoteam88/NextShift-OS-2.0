# Learning System Standard

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

Last Updated: 2026-07-02

---

## Purpose

Define the Learning System that continuously improves business recommendations, content quality, publishing strategy, and operational performance across NextShift OS.

---

## Mission

Transform every business action into reusable organizational intelligence.

The Learning System closes the AI Business Operating Loop by converting execution outcomes into better future decisions.

---

## Learning Loop

```text
Business Brain
  -> Decision
  -> Content Intelligence
  -> Workspace Execution
  -> Publishing
  -> Analytics
  -> Learning System
  -> Business Brain
```

---

## Learning Sources

## Business Signals

- Revenue
- CRM
- Pipeline
- Customer lifecycle
- Campaign outcomes

## Content Signals

- Reach
- Engagement
- Shares
- Saves
- Watch time
- Click-through rate

## Publishing Signals

- Publish time
- Platform
- Frequency
- Delivery success

## User Signals

- AI recommendations accepted
- AI recommendations rejected
- Manual edits
- Approval history

---

## Core Learning Engines

## Recommendation Learning

Improve recommendation quality using historical outcomes.

## Content Learning

Identify topics, formats, and messaging that consistently perform well.

## Publishing Learning

Optimize timing, platform selection, and scheduling.

## Business Learning

Detect recurring opportunities, risks, and operational bottlenecks.

---

## AI Responsibilities

AI should:

- Explain what changed
- Explain why it changed
- Recommend improvements
- Estimate future impact
- Increase recommendation confidence over time

---

## Governance Rules

The Learning System must:

- Never overwrite historical data.
- Preserve traceability.
- Use explainable recommendations.
- Respect business context and brand governance.

---

## Success Criteria

Every completed business action improves future recommendations, creating a measurable compounding effect across the Business Operating System.
