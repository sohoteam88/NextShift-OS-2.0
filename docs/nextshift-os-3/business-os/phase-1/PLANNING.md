# Business OS Phase 1 Planning

Version: v1.0
Status: Released

---

## Mission

Build the AI-native Business Operating System that enables NextShift to understand, manage, and execute business operations.

Completion of Phase 1 establishes the foundation for all future AI capabilities.

---

## Objectives

- Build a unified Business Operating System.
- Standardize business execution through AI Workflow.
- Unify decision making, automation, memory, and events.
- Integrate all existing business capabilities into one runtime.

---

## Execution Protocol

Each BOS capability follows the standard NextShift Project Execution Protocol:

1. Planning
2. Documentation Implementation Contract
3. Execution Task
4. Requirements Verification
5. Repository Audit
6. Release

---

## Phase Completion Criteria

Business OS Phase 1 is complete when:

- BOS-001 through BOS-008 are released.
- All modules are integrated.
- Runtime is stable.
- Documentation is complete.
- Repository passes verification and audit.
- Business OS v1.0 is released.
