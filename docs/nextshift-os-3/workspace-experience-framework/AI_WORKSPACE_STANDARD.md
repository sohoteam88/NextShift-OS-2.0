# AI Workspace Standard

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

Last Updated: 2026-07-02

---

## Purpose

Define how AI behaves consistently across every Workspace in NextShift OS.

---

## AI-First Principle

Every Workspace is AI-assisted by default.

AI is responsible for:

- Understanding context
- Generating recommendations
- Assisting execution
- Explaining decisions
- Learning from outcomes

The user always retains final approval.

---

## Standard AI Workflow

```text
Business Context
  -> Business Brain
  -> Workspace Context
  -> AI Recommendation
  -> User Review
  -> Execution
  -> Analytics
  -> Learning
```

---

## AI Responsibilities

## Business Understanding

- Read business context
- Understand customer goals
- Load workspace state

## Decision Support

- Recommend next actions
- Explain reasoning
- Estimate expected impact

## Creation

- Draft content
- Generate visuals
- Build workflows
- Produce business assets

## Optimization

- Detect bottlenecks
- Suggest improvements
- Learn from performance

---

## Human Responsibilities

Users remain responsible for:

- Strategic approval
- Brand decisions
- Compliance
- Final publishing
- Business ownership

---

## AI Interaction Rules

Every AI action should provide:

- Recommendation
- Reason
- Expected outcome
- Confidence
- One-click execution where appropriate

---

## Workspace Compatibility

This standard applies to:

- Business Workspace
- Content Workspace
- Visual Workspace
- Video Workspace
- Publishing Workspace
- Campaign Workspace
- CRM Workspace
- Analytics Workspace
- Admin Workspace

---

## Success Criteria

Users experience one consistent AI assistant across every NextShift Workspace, regardless of business function.
