# PHASE 8A.6 Growth Loop Authority Inventory Review

## Verdict

`APPROVE AS INVENTORY SCOPE, REJECT AS COMPLETED INVENTORY`

The brief defines the right authority question for V7.6:

```text
How does the system grow itself?
```

It also identifies the right domains to audit before migration planning:

- acquisition
- activation
- retention
- referral
- expansion

However, this file is only an inventory brief. It does not yet produce the required source inventory, duplicate authority map, or source summary.

## Why The Scope Is Correct

V7.6 Growth Loop should sit after execution and before future optimization planning.

The correct role is signal authority:

```text
execution outcomes
  -> performance signals
  -> growth findings
  -> optimization recommendations
  -> upstream engines decide what changes
```

Growth Loop must not become a second Journey Engine, mission planner, CRM engine, or Business State writer.

## Current Repo Reality

There is no single canonical Growth Loop authority yet.

Growth behavior is distributed across multiple module-local systems:

- acquisition logic exists in lead, funnel, traffic, content, and CRM surfaces
- activation logic exists in activation, onboarding, journey, mission, and revenue surfaces
- retention logic exists in CRM followups, WhatsApp followups, automation workflows, analytics, team, and franchise surfaces
- referral logic exists in member invite services, invite APIs, and join flows
- expansion logic exists in admin platform metrics, team/franchise growth metrics, business intelligence, automation, and upgrade/funnel monitoring

That fragmentation is exactly why this inventory phase is needed.

## What Is Missing From The Brief

Before implementation planning, the inventory must add:

- file-level source inventory
- read/write classification for each source
- source-of-truth candidate for each growth domain
- duplicate authority findings
- downstream consumer list
- precedence rules
- migration risk level
- recommended retirement or bridge strategy for non-canonical sources
