# RFC-0003 - AI Operating Loop

Status: Approved

Current document: [0.3 AI Operating Loop](../phase-0-foundation/0.3_AI_OPERATING_LOOP.md)

## Summary

Define the approved cognitive workflow for AI inside NextShift OS 3.0.

## Decision

AI capabilities must follow the operating loop: Observe, Understand, Recommend, Discuss, Decide, Execute, Measure, Reflect, and Learn.

## Constitution Impact

This RFC establishes the required flow for AI reasoning, collaboration, execution, and learning.

## Approval Criteria

- Requires understanding before action.
- Preserves human decision ownership.
- Makes learning part of every completed loop.
