# NextShift OS PRD Master

## Product Goal

NextShift OS is an AI-guided operating system that helps new online business users build their personal brand, create content, capture leads, follow up through CRM and WhatsApp, and convert through funnels.

## Target Users

- New online business users
- Coaches, creators, consultants, and small teams
- Operators who need a guided daily workflow instead of a blank SaaS dashboard

## Core Promise

Help a beginner know what to do today, complete one meaningful business action, and move from traffic to lead to conversion.

## Product Pillars

- Mission Engine
- Brand Discovery
- Content Engine
- Funnel Builder
- WhatsApp AI
- CRM
- Analytics
- Admin and SaaS Layer

## Success Metrics

- Onboarding completion rate
- First mission completion rate
- First content published
- First funnel launched
- Lead captured
- Follow-up sent
- Consultation booked
- Activation within 5 minutes
