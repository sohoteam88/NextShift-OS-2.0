# Visual Workspace Standard

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

Last Updated: 2026-07-02

---

## Purpose

Define the standard architecture, workflow, and governance for the Visual Workspace within NextShift OS.

---

## Mission

The Visual Workspace transforms business strategy and content briefs into branded visual assets.

Content Intelligence decides what visuals are needed.

The Visual Workspace creates, edits, reviews, and prepares them for publishing.

---

## End-To-End Workflow

```text
Business Brain
  -> Content Intelligence
  -> Content Brief
  -> Visual Workspace
  -> Review
  -> Publish
  -> Analytics
  -> Learning
```

---

## Export Center

- Multi-platform resize
- Format conversion
- Draft export
- Publishing handoff

---

## AI Assistance Rules

Every generated visual should include:

- Design rationale
- Brand compliance status
- Recommended platforms
- Suggested improvements
- Confidence score

Users always retain final approval.

---

## Governance Rules

The Visual Workspace must:

- Reuse the Design System and UI Kit.
- Respect Brand Governance.
- Consume Content Intelligence outputs.
- Record asset performance for the Learning System.

---

## Success Criteria

Users can create, edit, review, brand, resize, and prepare visual assets for multiple platforms without leaving NextShift.
