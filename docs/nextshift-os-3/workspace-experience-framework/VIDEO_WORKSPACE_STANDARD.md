# Video Workspace Standard

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

Last Updated: 2026-07-02

---

## Purpose

Define the architecture, workflow, and governance for the Video Workspace in NextShift OS.

---

## Mission

The Video Workspace converts business strategy and approved content briefs into short-form and long-form videos ready for publication.

Content Intelligence determines what videos should be created.

The Video Workspace produces, edits, reviews, and exports them.

---

## Workflow

```text
Business Brain
  -> Content Intelligence
  -> Content Brief
  -> Video Workspace
  -> Review
  -> Publish
  -> Analytics
  -> Learning
```

---

## Core Modules

## Video Brief

- Business objective

- Platform
- Video length
- CTA
- KPI

- Hook generation

- Platform adaptation

- Scene planning
- Storyboard

- B-roll suggestions
- AI video prompt generation

## Video Editing

- Timeline editing
- Scene replacement
- Caption editing
- Music selection
- Thumbnail generation
- Multi-format export

## Publishing

- Approval workflow
- Scheduling
- Multi-platform publishing
- Performance tracking

---

## AI Assistance Rules

Every AI recommendation should include:

- Production rationale
- Expected engagement
- Suggested duration
- Platform recommendation
- Confidence score

Users retain final publishing approval.

---

## Governance Rules

The Video Workspace must:

- Consume Content Intelligence outputs.
- Reuse Design System, UI Kit, and Workspace Experience Framework.
- Respect Brand Governance.
- Feed analytics back into the Learning System.

---

## Success Criteria

Users can create, edit, review, optimize, and publish professional videos for multiple platforms without leaving NextShift.
