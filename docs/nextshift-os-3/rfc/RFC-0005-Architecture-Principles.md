# RFC-0005 - Architecture Principles

Status: Approved

Current document: [0.5 Architecture Principles](../phase-0-foundation/0.5_ARCHITECTURE_PRINCIPLES.md)

## Summary

Define the architectural principles that guide NextShift OS 3.0 product design, software engineering, AI reasoning, database design, APIs, and user experience.

## Decision

Architecture must be AI First, Business Twin First, decision-led, capability-oriented, shared-intelligence based, learning-oriented, explainable, context-preserving, separated by responsibility, and designed for evolution.

## Constitution Impact

This RFC establishes architectural review criteria for all future capabilities.

## Approval Criteria

- Strengthens the Business Twin.
- Improves decision quality.
- Follows the AI Operating Loop.
- Avoids duplicated business knowledge.
- Keeps reasoning explainable.
