# Creative Studio Architecture

Version: 1.0

Status: Implemented

Last Updated: 2026-07-08

---

## Purpose

Define how NextShift creates, edits, approves, and packages business creative assets.

Creative Studio turns approved business intent into content, visual, video, and publishing packages.

---

## Responsibilities

| Area | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Content Generation | Produce posts, scripts, captions, outlines, campaigns, and messaging |
| Visual Generation | Produce creative briefs, image concepts, visual directions, and asset variants |
| Video Generation | Produce video concepts, scripts, shot plans, and publishing-ready structures |
| Publishing Package | Package approved assets for channels and campaigns |
| Approval Workflow | Support review, revision, approval, and rejection |
| Workspace Integration | Connect creation to workspace state and asset history |

---

## Inputs

Creative Studio reads:

- Conversation Engine briefs
- Decision Engine recommendations
- Business Brain context
- Brand DNA
- customer intelligence
- channel requirements
- campaign goals

---

## Outputs

Creative Studio produces:

- content drafts
- creative briefs
- visual package definitions
- video package definitions
- publishing packages
- approval state
- revision history

---

## Boundary

Creative Studio creates and packages assets.

It does not own recommendation priority, durable business identity, CRM conversion handling, or revenue forecasting.
