# Content Intelligence Standard

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

Last Updated: 2026-07-02

---

## Purpose

Define the intelligence layer that enables NextShift to decide what content should be created, why it should be created, and when it should be published.

---

## Mission

Content Intelligence transforms business context and market signals into actionable content recommendations.

It supports MVP Phase 3.

---

## Intelligence Pipeline

```text
Business Context
  -> CRM Signals
  -> Campaign Status
  -> Revenue Goals
  -> Market Trends
  -> Competitor Intelligence
  -> Opportunity Detection
  -> Content Recommendation
  -> Content Brief
  -> Content Workspace
```

---

## Core Engines

## Trend Intelligence

- Search trends
- Social trends
- Seasonal events
- Industry signals

## Viral Discovery

- Emerging topics
- Viral patterns
- Engagement signals

## Competitor Intelligence

- Content themes
- Posting frequency
- Engagement comparison
- Opportunity gaps

## Audience Intelligence

- Customer questions
- Pain points
- Behaviour changes
- Intent signals

## Business Intelligence

Combine:

- Business Brain
- CRM
- Campaign
- Analytics
- Revenue
- Goals

to prioritize content by expected business impact.

---

## Required Outputs

Every recommendation should include:

- Topic
- Business objective
- Target audience
- Recommended platforms
- Suggested format
- Expected KPI
- Confidence score

---

## Governance Rules

Content recommendations must:

- Support business goals
- Be evidence-based
- Preserve brand consistency
- Feed the Learning System after publishing

---

## Success Criteria

The AI consistently recommends content with measurable business value instead of generating content from isolated prompts.
