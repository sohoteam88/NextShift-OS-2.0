# Document 0.6 - AI Reasoning Model

Version 1.0

## Purpose

This document defines how AI transforms business information into business intelligence.

The Reasoning Model standardizes how all AI Agents observe, understand, recommend, and learn.

Every recommendation generated by NextShift must follow this reasoning process.

## Objective

The purpose of AI is not to generate content.

The purpose of AI is to generate better business decisions.

Reasoning bridges the gap between information and action.

## The Reasoning Pipeline

```text
Business Signals
  -> Business Context
  -> Business Understanding
  -> Insights
  -> Opportunities
  -> Recommendations
  -> Discussion
  -> Decision
  -> Execution
  -> Reflection
  -> Learning
```

## Stage 1 - Observe

AI collects business signals.

Examples:

- Sales activity
- Customer conversations
- Marketing performance
- Financial metrics
- User interactions

Output:

Business Signals

## Stage 2 - Understand

AI combines signals with the Business Twin.

Questions include:

- What changed?
- Why did it change?
- What is the current business state?

Output:

Business Understanding

## Stage 3 - Generate Insights

AI identifies meaningful patterns.

Examples:

- Conversion rate declined.
- Customer retention improved.
- Lead quality increased.

Insights explain observations rather than merely reporting data.

## Stage 4 - Discover Opportunities

AI evaluates where improvements are possible.

Each opportunity includes:

- Potential value
- Estimated effort
- Risk
- Strategic alignment

## Stage 5 - Produce Recommendations

Recommendations are ranked according to:

- Business impact
- Confidence
- Urgency
- Strategic fit
- Resource requirements

Every recommendation should answer:

- Why now?
- Why this?
- Why not another option?

## Stage 6 - Discussion

AI collaborates with the entrepreneur.

The entrepreneur may:

- Ask questions
- Challenge assumptions
- Provide missing context
- Reject recommendations
- Modify recommendations

Discussion improves reasoning quality.

## Stage 7 - Decision

The entrepreneur approves, modifies, or rejects recommendations.

Every decision records:

- Decision
- Rationale
- Timestamp
- Expected outcome

Decision history becomes Business Memory.

## Stage 8 - Execution

Approved decisions become executable actions.

Execution Capabilities perform the work.

Examples:

- Publish content
- Launch campaigns
- Update CRM
- Send WhatsApp
- Build landing pages

## Stage 9 - Reflection

After execution, AI evaluates:

- Expected outcome
- Actual outcome
- Success factors
- Failure factors
- Unexpected observations

Reflection creates business insights.

## Stage 10 - Learning

Reflection permanently updates:

- Business Twin
- Business Memory
- Knowledge Graph
- Recommendation Models

Future recommendations become more accurate.

## Confidence Model

Every recommendation should include a confidence level based on:

- Data quality
- Historical evidence
- Similar business cases
- Completeness of context
- Business uncertainty

When confidence is low, AI should ask clarifying questions instead of making strong recommendations.

## Core Principle

NextShift does not reason from isolated data.

It reasons from the evolving Business Twin.

Business understanding always precedes business action.
