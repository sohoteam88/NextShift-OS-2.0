# Decision Brain Contract

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

## Purpose

This document defines the contractual responsibilities of the Decision Brain.

The Decision Brain transforms business understanding into intelligent business decisions.

It is responsible for evaluating opportunities, assessing risks, prioritizing actions, and generating explainable recommendations.

The Decision Brain is the only provider of business judgment within NextShift OS.

## Mission

The mission of the Decision Brain is to maximize long-term business success by consistently recommending the highest-value actions.

The Decision Brain converts understanding into action.

## Definition

The Decision Brain is the cognitive decision layer of NextShift.

It consumes business understanding from the Business Brain and produces recommendations that guide execution.

It does not execute work.

It determines what should happen next.

## Contract Principles

The Decision Brain must be:

- Explainable
- Context-aware
- Goal-oriented
- Evidence-based
- Risk-aware
- Adaptive
- Learning-driven

## Responsibilities

The Decision Brain shall:

- Evaluate opportunities.
- Assess risks.
- Prioritize actions.
- Generate recommendations.
- Explain reasoning.
- Coordinate strategic thinking.
- Support entrepreneur decisions.
- Improve recommendation quality through learning.

## Internal Components

The Decision Brain consists of:

- Recommendation Engine
- Strategy Engine
- Opportunity Engine
- Risk Engine
- Prioritization Engine
- Conversation Engine

These components work together to produce a single decision context.

## Required Capabilities

The Decision Brain must provide the following capabilities.

### Recommendation Generation

Produce ranked recommendations aligned with business goals.

### Opportunity Evaluation

Identify potential business improvements.

### Risk Assessment

Evaluate uncertainty, constraints, and potential negative outcomes.

### Strategic Alignment

Ensure recommendations support long-term business strategy.

### Priority Ranking

Rank recommendations according to:

- Business impact
- Strategic alignment
- Urgency
- Confidence
- Resource availability
- Risk

### Decision Explanation

Every recommendation should explain:

- Why
- Why now
- Expected impact
- Supporting evidence
- Risks
- Alternatives
- Confidence level

### Decision Context

Provide complete decision context for AI Agents and the entrepreneur.

## Inputs

The Decision Brain receives:

- Business Twin
- Business Intelligence
- Business Memory
- Story Vault
- Knowledge Graph
- Business Goals
- Current Priorities
- User Feedback
- Learning Signals

## Outputs

The Decision Brain provides:

- Ranked Recommendations
- Opportunity Lists
- Risk Assessments
- Strategic Guidance
- Decision Context
- Priority Queue
- Recommendation Explanations

## Consumers

The Decision Brain serves:

- AI Agents
- AI Coach
- Execution Layer
- Capability Layer
- Entrepreneur
- Notification System

## Prohibited Responsibilities

The Decision Brain must not:

- Execute workflows.
- Modify Business Memory directly.
- Replace the Business Brain.
- Own business knowledge.
- Store presentation state.
- Perform execution-specific logic.

Judgment belongs here.

Execution belongs elsewhere.

## Architectural Rules

### Rule 1

Every recommendation must reference Business Brain context.

### Rule 2

Recommendations must be explainable.

### Rule 3

Priority should reflect business value rather than technical convenience.

### Rule 4

Rejected recommendations should become learning signals.

### Rule 5

Decision quality is more important than recommendation quantity.

### Rule 6

The Decision Brain should improve continuously through Learning System feedback.

## Success Criteria

The Decision Brain Contract is satisfied when:

- Recommendations are context-aware.
- Business priorities remain aligned with strategy.
- AI reasoning is explainable.
- Entrepreneurs consistently receive valuable guidance.
- Decision quality improves over time.

## Guiding Principle

The Business Brain understands the business.

The Decision Brain determines what should happen next.

Together they form the intelligence core of NextShift OS.
