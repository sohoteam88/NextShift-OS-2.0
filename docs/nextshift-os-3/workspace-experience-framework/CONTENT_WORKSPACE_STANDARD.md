# Content Workspace Standard

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

Last Updated: 2026-07-02

---

## Purpose

Define the standard architecture, responsibilities, and workflow for the Content Workspace in NextShift OS.

---

## Mission

The Content Workspace is where business strategy becomes publishable content.

It does not decide what to create.

Content Intelligence decides.

Content Workspace executes.

---

## End-To-End Workflow

```text
Business Brain
  -> Content Intelligence
  -> Content Brief
  -> Content Workspace
  -> Review
  -> Publish
  -> Analytics
  -> Learning
```

---

---

## AI Assistance Rules

AI should always provide:

- Recommendation
- Reasoning
- Expected KPI
- Confidence score
- One-click improvements

Users retain final approval.

---

## Governance Rules

The Content Workspace:

- consumes Content Intelligence;
- reuses the Design System, UI Kit, and Workspace Experience Framework;
- never bypasses Brand Governance;
- records publishing outcomes for the Learning System.

---

## Success Criteria

Users can move from AI-generated content brief to reviewed, branded, multi-platform publication inside a single workspace.
