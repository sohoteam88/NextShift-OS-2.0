# Business Brain Architecture

Version: 1.0

Status: Implemented

Last Updated: 2026-07-08

---

## Purpose

Define how NextShift understands the business.

Business Brain translates Business Foundation state into business context, reasoning inputs, and insight.

---

## Responsibilities

| Area | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Business Twin | Maintain the current representation of the business |
| Business Memory | Retrieve durable facts, context, and prior decisions |
| Knowledge Graph | Relate entities, offers, customers, channels, and outcomes |
| Story Vault | Preserve narrative, proof, positioning, and reusable story context |
| Brand DNA | Represent voice, positioning, promise, values, and differentiation |
| Customer Intelligence | Represent segments, needs, objections, signals, and behavior |
| Insight | Produce context-rich understanding for decisions and execution |

---

## Inputs

Business Brain reads:

- Business Foundation identity and profile state
- memory, knowledge, story, timeline, learning, and reflection records
- workflow outputs
- customer, offer, channel, campaign, and revenue signals

---

## Outputs

Business Brain produces:

- business context
- insight
- constraints
- evidence summaries
- customer and offer understanding
- confidence inputs for Decision Engine

---

## Boundary

Business Brain understands and reasons about business context.

It does not approve actions, execute workflows, generate final assets, or own daily mission presentation.
