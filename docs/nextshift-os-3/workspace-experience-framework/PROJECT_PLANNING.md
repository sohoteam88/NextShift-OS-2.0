## Vision

Workspace Experience Framework v1.0 defines the Workspace operating model for every current and future NextShift Business OS.

The framework becomes the single source of truth for Workspace experience decisions across Product Architecture, Documentation Engineering, Audit Engineering, Frontend Engineering, QA, future Business Capabilities, and future Business OS work.

WEF does not redefine Design System implementation, UI Kit guidance, runtime architecture, or business capability behavior. It defines reusable Workspace specifications that sit above UI guidance and below business-specific product workflows.
