# Beta User Interview Template

## Purpose

Validate whether beta users can achieve real outcomes with NextShift OS and identify what blocks them.

This interview is not a feature satisfaction survey. It is an outcome validation interview.

## Interview Cadence

| Interview | Timing | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 1 | Within 24 hours of signup | Find activation confusion and first-action friction |
| Day 7 | After one week | Validate retention, first lead progress, and AI COO usefulness |
| Day 30 | End of beta window | Validate value realization, expansion, referral, and willingness to continue |

## Day 1 Interview

### Core Questions

1. What confused you?
2. What helped you most?
3. What nearly caused you to quit?
4. What result did you achieve?

### Activation Questions

1. Did you understand why the interview mattered?
2. Did the Brand DNA output feel accurate enough to use?
3. Did you know what to do after the interview?
4. How long did it take before you saw something useful?
5. Could you explain the dashboard's next action in your own words?

## Day 7 Interview

### Core Questions

1. What confused you this week?
2. What helped you most this week?
3. What nearly caused you to stop using it?
4. What result did you achieve?

### Retention and Value Questions

1. What made you come back?
2. What did you ignore or avoid?
3. Did NextShift help you get closer to a lead, customer, sale, recruit, or audience result?
4. Was the AI COO recommendation useful, wrong, too vague, or too much?
5. Did you trust the dashboard's suggested next action?
6. What did you still need human help for?

## Day 30 Interview

### Core Questions

1. What confused you most across the month?
2. What helped you most across the month?
3. What nearly caused you to quit?
4. What result did you achieve?

### Outcome Validation Questions

1. Did you achieve a meaningful business result?
2. Which result mattered most: content, lead, customer, sale, recruit, audience, or referral?
3. Did NextShift help you repeat or scale that result?
4. Did AI COO recommendations improve your business progress?
5. Would you recommend this to someone similar to you?
6. What would need to change before you would pay or keep using it?
