# Retention Marketer Checklist

Before finishing a `retention-marketer` task, check:

- Customer lifecycle stages are clear.
- Segments include new, active, at-risk, dormant, and VIP/referral customers.
- Follow-up starts with customer value, not only promotion.
- Reactivation includes a clear trigger and message sequence.
- Referral flow has timing, copy, and tracking.
- Retention metrics are defined.
- No spammy pressure, fake urgency, or unsafe claims are used.
- The final response summarizes files changed, what was added, and what still needs improvement when code or files were edited.
