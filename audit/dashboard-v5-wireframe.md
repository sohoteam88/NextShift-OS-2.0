# Dashboard V5 Wireframe

## Objective

Turn the member Dashboard into Mission Control.

## Layout

### 1. Today's Mission

Full-width hero card.

- Current mission title
- Why it matters
- Expected outcome
- Estimated time
- Primary CTA: Continue Mission

### 2. Progress Path

Six-step path only.

1. Profile
2. Brand Interview
3. First Content
4. Lead Magnet
5. Landing Page
6. Launch

States:

- Completed: check mark
- Current: highlighted
- Future: locked

### 3. AI COO Recommendation

Single recommendation only.

- Recommendation title
- Reason
- Expected outcome
- CTA: Execute

### 4. Business Snapshot

Four KPIs only.

- Readiness
- Progress
- Growth
- Leads

No charts. No analytics walls.

### 5. Quick Access

Collapsed by default.

- Content Engine
- Funnel Builder
- AI Workforce
- Analytics
- Settings

Locked items remain visible but disabled.
