# Email Automation Examples

## Example 1: Lead Magnet Delivery Sequence

**Input:** "Design email sequence after someone downloads my '7-Day Meal Prep Guide'."

**Expected output:**

5-email sequence over 10 days:
1. Day 0: Delivery email (download link + "如何使用这份指南")
2. Day 2: Value email (meal prep tip #1 + success story)
3. Day 4: Education email (common mistake + how to fix)
4. Day 7: Soft pitch (free consultation offer + social proof)
5. Day 10: Last chance (deadline + WhatsApp CTA)

Subject lines in Chinese. Segment: if opened email 4 but didn't click → send follow-up. If clicked → tag as warm lead in CRM.

## Example 2: Dormant Customer Reactivation

**Input:** "Design reactivation emails for customers who haven't reordered in 60 days."

**Expected output:** 3-email sequence: check-in ("最近还好吗？"), value reminder (results others are getting), special offer (limited bundle). Exit criteria: reorder or unsubscribe.

## When NOT to Use This Skill

- User needs **WhatsApp automation** → use `ai/whatsapp-automation`
- User needs **email template design** (HTML/CSS) → use `core/design-system-architect`
