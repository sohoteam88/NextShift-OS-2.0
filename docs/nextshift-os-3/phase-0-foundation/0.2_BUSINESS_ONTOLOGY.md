# Document 0.2 - NextShift Business Ontology

Version 1.0

## Purpose

This document defines the official language of NextShift OS.

Every AI Agent, developer, designer, architect, and future document must use these definitions consistently.

If a concept is defined here, no alternative definition should be introduced elsewhere.

## 1. Business

### Definition

A Business is a living system that creates value by transforming resources into outcomes for customers.

A business is not merely a company, a legal entity, or a collection of software tools.

## 2. Business Twin

### Definition

A Business Twin is the AI's continuously evolving digital understanding of a business.

It represents how the AI understands the business, not how the database stores it.

### Includes

- Goals
- Vision
- Strategy
- Products
- Customers
- Brand
- Operations
- Financial Signals
- History
- Decisions
- Knowledge
- Memory
- Relationships

### Does Not Include

- UI Components
- Database Tables
- CRM Records
- Individual Documents

## 3. Story

### Definition

A Story is a meaningful business event that changes business understanding.

Stories provide context rather than raw information.

Examples:

- Product launched
- Customer complaint resolved
- Sales campaign failed
- Investor meeting completed

## 4. Memory

### Definition

Memory is persistent business experience retained across time.

Memory allows AI to remember what matters.

Examples:

- User preferences
- Historical decisions
- Brand voice
- Successful campaigns
- Failed experiments

## 5. Knowledge

### Definition

Knowledge is verified understanding created by connecting multiple stories, memories, and facts.

Knowledge explains why, not only what.

## 6. Knowledge Graph

### Definition

The Knowledge Graph represents relationships between business entities.

It answers questions such as:

- How are these connected?
- What influences what?
- What dependencies exist?

## 7. Recommendation

### Definition

A Recommendation is an AI-generated proposed action designed to improve business outcomes.

Every recommendation should include:

- Reason
- Expected impact
- Confidence
- Supporting evidence
- Risks

## 8. Decision

### Definition

A Decision is a confirmed business choice made by the entrepreneur or an approved automation policy.

Every decision becomes part of Business Memory.

## 9. Capability

### Definition

A Capability is something NextShift can perform.

Examples:

- Generate content
- Build landing pages
- Send WhatsApp
- Launch campaigns
- Analyze sales

Capabilities execute work.

They do not decide strategy.

## 10. Agent

### Definition

An Agent is an AI specialist responsible for one business responsibility.

Agents collaborate through the Business Twin.

Agents never own business truth independently.

## 11. Insight

### Definition

An Insight is a meaningful understanding discovered from data.

Insights explain patterns rather than merely reporting metrics.

## 12. Opportunity

### Definition

An Opportunity is a potential action that could improve business performance.

Opportunities are discovered before recommendations are generated.

## 13. Reflection

### Definition

Reflection is the process of evaluating outcomes after execution.

Reflection identifies:

- Success
- Failure
- Unexpected results
- Lessons learned

## 14. Learning

### Definition

Learning is the permanent improvement of the Business Twin based on reflection.

Learning changes future recommendations.

## 15. Execution

### Definition

Execution is the implementation of an approved decision.

Execution never occurs before a decision unless explicitly authorized.

## 16. Signal

### Definition

A Signal is any observable change in the business environment.

Signals are raw inputs awaiting interpretation.

Examples:

- Sales increased
- Customer replied
- Website traffic dropped
- New lead created

## 17. Goal

### Definition

A Goal is a measurable business outcome the entrepreneur intends to achieve.

Goals guide prioritization across the entire operating system.

## Fundamental Rule

Everything inside NextShift must ultimately strengthen one or more of these concepts.

If a new feature cannot be mapped to these concepts, it does not belong in the platform.
