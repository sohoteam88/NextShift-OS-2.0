# Business OS Phase 1 Execution Plan

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

Last Updated: 2026-07-02

---

## Purpose

Provide the execution sequence for Business OS Phase 1: Business OS Foundation.

This document is a Business OS Phase 1 execution plan. It is subordinate to the MVP 1.0 Implementation Master Plan and does not replace MVP-level sequencing authority.

---

## Objective

Deliver a complete Business Operating Foundation capable of understanding business context, generating explainable recommendations, and supporting all downstream workspaces.

---

## Execution Order

## Milestone 1 - Core Business Context

- Business Profile
- Business Twin
- Business Goals
- Brand DNA

### Exit Gate

- Business identity complete
- Business context queryable

---

## Milestone 2 - Customer Intelligence

- CRM
- Customer Lifecycle
- Segmentation
- Follow-up foundation

### Exit Gate

- Customer context available to Business Brain

---

## Milestone 3 - Commercial Intelligence

- Campaign
- Revenue
- Analytics
- KPI framework

### Exit Gate

- Business performance measurable

---

## Milestone 4 - Cognitive Layer

- Decision Brain
- Business Brain
- AI Workflow
- Knowledge Graph
- Story Vault
- Business Memory

### Exit Gate

- Explainable recommendations
- Cross-capability context sharing

---

## Mandatory Deliverables

Each milestone must include:

- Planning
- Implementation
- Verification
- Audit
- GitHub Alignment
- Release Package

---

## Dependencies

No Phase 2 Workspace implementation may bypass unfinished Phase 1 milestones.

---

## Completion Criteria

Phase 1 is complete only when:

- Business Brain operational
- Recommendations explainable
- Business context reusable
- GitHub aligned
- Governance passed
