# RFC-0001 - First Principles

Status: Approved

Current document: [0.1 First Principles](../phase-0-foundation/0.1_FIRST_PRINCIPLES.md)

## Summary

Establish the immutable first principles for NextShift OS 3.0.

## Decision

NextShift is an AI Guided Business Operating System designed to improve business decisions through the Business Twin and AI Operating Loop.

## Constitution Impact

This RFC establishes Phase 0 First Principles as a foundation document.

## Approval Criteria

- Aligns with the Business Twin model.
- Defines decision quality as the primary purpose.
- Separates execution capabilities from the product itself.
- Requires explainable recommendations and human-owned decisions.
