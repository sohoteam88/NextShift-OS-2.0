# Story Vault Contract

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

## Purpose

This document defines the contractual responsibilities of the Story Vault.

The Story Vault preserves meaningful business narratives that provide context for reasoning, learning, and future decision-making.

The Story Vault stores stories.

It does not store every event.

## Mission

The mission of the Story Vault is to preserve the business narrative.

Stories explain how the business evolves.

Stories provide context that cannot be derived from isolated events or database records.

## Definition

A Story is a meaningful sequence of business events that together represent a business outcome, milestone, challenge, or learning experience.

Stories answer:

- What happened?
- Why did it matter?

## Contract Principles

The Story Vault must be:

- Narrative-driven
- Chronological
- Context-rich
- Immutable
- Explainable
- Searchable

## Responsibilities

The Story Vault shall:

- Preserve significant business stories.
- Connect related business events.
- Provide historical business context.
- Support Business Twin reasoning.
- Support Learning.
- Support AI conversations.

## Story Sources

Stories may originate from:

- Business Events
- Decisions
- Campaigns
- Customer Journeys
- Product Launches
- AI Conversations
- User Reflections
- Learning Records

Stories should combine related information into coherent narratives.

## Required Capabilities

The Story Vault must provide the following capabilities.

### Story Creation

Create stories from meaningful business activity.

### Story Retrieval

Retrieve relevant stories based on business context.

### Story Linking

Connect stories to:

- Business Twin
- Customers
- Products
- Campaigns
- Decisions
- Goals

### Story Timeline

Provide chronological business history.

### Story Search

Support semantic and contextual retrieval.

### Story Summarization

Generate concise summaries while preserving the underlying narrative.

## Story Structure

Every Story should include:

- Story ID
- Title
- Summary
- Timeline
- Related Events
- Related Decisions
- Related Entities
- Business Impact
- Lessons Learned
- Tags

## Integration Contract

The Story Vault receives information from:

- Event Architecture
- Learning System
- Decision Records
- User Conversations
- AI Observations

The Story Vault provides information to:

- Business Twin
- Business Brain
- AI Agents
- Decision Brain
- AI Coach

## Prohibited Responsibilities

The Story Vault must not:

- Replace Business Memory.
- Replace Knowledge Graph.
- Replace analytics.
- Execute workflows.
- Store presentation state.

## Success Criteria

The Story Vault Contract is satisfied when:

- Business history remains understandable.
- AI can explain important business milestones.
- Relevant stories improve reasoning.
- Stories strengthen the Business Twin.
- Historical context survives beyond individual conversations.

## Guiding Principle

Events describe what happened.

Stories explain why it mattered.

The Story Vault preserves the narrative of the business.
