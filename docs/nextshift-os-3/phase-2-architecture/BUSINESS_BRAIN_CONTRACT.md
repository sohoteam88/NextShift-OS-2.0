# Business Brain Contract

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

## Purpose

This document defines the contractual responsibilities of the Business Brain.

The Business Brain is the cognitive foundation of NextShift OS.

It is responsible for maintaining a complete, continuously evolving understanding of the business.

The Business Brain is the single provider of business context for every intelligent component.

## Mission

The mission of the Business Brain is to transform business information into business understanding.

It enables every AI component to reason from shared business intelligence rather than isolated data.

## Definition

The Business Brain is the cognitive layer responsible for understanding the business.

It integrates identity, memory, stories, knowledge, relationships, customer context, strategy, and historical experience into a unified business understanding.

## Contract Principles

The Business Brain must be:

- Shared
- Persistent
- Explainable
- Context-aware
- Continuously evolving
- Independent of implementation technology

## Responsibilities

The Business Brain shall:

- Maintain business understanding.
- Build and evolve the Business Twin.
- Provide contextual intelligence.
- Preserve business continuity.
- Support reasoning.
- Support recommendations.
- Support learning.
- Coordinate business knowledge.

## Internal Components

The Business Brain is composed of:

- Business Twin
- Business Memory
- Story Vault
- Knowledge Graph
- Brand DNA
- Customer Intelligence

These components work together to produce one coherent business understanding.

## Required Capabilities

The Business Brain must provide the following capabilities.

### Business Context

Provide complete business context for AI reasoning.

### Historical Context

Provide historical continuity across conversations and decisions.

### Knowledge Retrieval

Retrieve relevant knowledge based on current business context.

### Story Retrieval

Retrieve meaningful business stories.

### Memory Retrieval

Retrieve important business experience.

### Relationship Retrieval

Retrieve entity relationships from the Knowledge Graph.

### Context Assembly

Assemble information from multiple sources into a unified business context.

### Business Understanding

Provide a single coherent understanding of the business.

## Inputs

The Business Brain receives information from:

- Business Events
- Story Vault
- Business Memory
- Knowledge Graph
- Learning System
- User Conversations
- External Systems
- Business Signals

## Outputs

The Business Brain provides:

- Business Twin context
- Business Intelligence
- AI Context
- Recommendation Context
- Strategy Context
- Customer Context
- Brand Context
- Historical Context

Every AI component should consume Business Brain outputs rather than querying individual subsystems directly.

## Consumers

The Business Brain serves:

- Decision Brain
- AI Agents
- AI Coach
- Recommendation Engine
- Opportunity Engine
- Strategy Engine
- Analytics
- Execution Layer

## Prohibited Responsibilities

The Business Brain must not:

- Execute workflows.
- Prioritize decisions.
- Approve recommendations.
- Replace the Decision Brain.
- Own execution capabilities.
- Own presentation logic.

Understanding belongs here.

Decision-making belongs elsewhere.

## Architectural Rules

### Rule 1

The Business Brain is the only provider of unified business context.

### Rule 2

Subsystems should strengthen the Business Brain rather than compete with it.

### Rule 3

Business understanding should remain consistent across all AI components.

### Rule 4

Every learning event should improve the Business Brain.

### Rule 5

The Business Brain should remain independent of UI, APIs, and storage technologies.

## Success Criteria

The Business Brain Contract is satisfied when:

- All AI components share consistent business understanding.
- Business context is assembled from multiple knowledge sources.
- Recommendations become increasingly context-aware.
- Historical continuity is preserved.
- Business understanding improves continuously.

## Guiding Principle

The Business Brain does not make decisions.

It makes intelligent decisions possible.
