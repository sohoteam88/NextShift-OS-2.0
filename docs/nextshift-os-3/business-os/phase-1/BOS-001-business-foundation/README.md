# BOS-001 Business Foundation

Version: v1.0
Status: Released

## Purpose

BOS-001 Business Foundation consolidates the existing business capability documentation into the canonical foundation for Business OS Phase 1.

The foundation defines how CAP-001 through CAP-008 relate to the unified Business Operating System before BOS-002 Decision Intelligence begins.

## Scope

BOS-001 covers:

- CAP-001 Business Profile
- CAP-002 CRM
- CAP-003 Content
- CAP-004 Campaign
- CAP-005 Revenue Forecast
- CAP-006 Analytics
- CAP-007 Decision Brain
- CAP-008 Business Brain

## Foundation Rule

Business OS Phase 1 must treat CAP-001 through CAP-008 as one coordinated business foundation. Individual capabilities may keep their existing lifecycle records, but Business OS owns the integration narrative, dependency model, and preparation path for BOS-002.
